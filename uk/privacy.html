Privacy

Privacy and Business Contact Notice

This notice explains, in plain English, what personal data FreightDocDesk handles, where it comes from, why we use it and how to make us stop. It covers our business emails to UK road forwarders, the file details our clients share with us during a pilot or monthly plan, and this website.

Last updated: 26 November 2026

It gives the information UK GDPR Art. 13 and Art. 14 require, including where we did not collect your details from you directly, and explains how we follow the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR).

The short version

  • We email named people at UK road forwarding companies about our carrier paperwork desk. We find their work details in public business sources, the Companies House register and business contact data providers.
  • To stop, reply "stop" to any of our emails or write to desk@freightdocdesk.com. We stop all outreach to you.
  • Client file details are only handled under a signed UK GDPR Art. 28 DPA, used only to chase delivery paperwork, and deleted within 30 days of the pilot or plan ending.
  • We never sell personal data.

A. Business contact outreach

This part applies if you received a first email from us about FreightDocDesk. Our business outreach is sent from freightdocops.com and freightdocmail.com. This site, client replies and pilot operations use freightdocdesk.com.

What we hold about you

  • Your name, job title and the company you work for.
  • Your work email address, and your work phone number where your company publishes it.
  • Company details such as the Companies House company number and registered office.
  • The public source where we found the details, and the date.
  • A record of the emails we sent you and any reply, including an opt-out.

We do not hold home addresses, personal email addresses or any special category data.

Where it came from

We did not get your details from you, so this part gives the information UK GDPR Art. 14 requires. We collected them from publicly available business sources and from business contact data providers:

  • Public company websites, including team pages, published job listings and published trading terms.
  • The Companies House register.
  • Business contact data providers Apollo (apollo.io) and Prospeo (prospeo.io), which supply and verify work email addresses and job titles.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

We use these details to send a small number of plain, relevant emails to people whose role covers operations, traffic, accounts or billing, about a service that recovers delivery paperwork for their business. Our lawful basis under UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(f) is legitimate interests: telling road forwarders about a service that is directly relevant to their work.

We have weighed that interest against yours. We only use work contact details from public business sources and business contact data providers, only write about work matters, keep volumes low and stop at once when asked. You can ask for a copy of our assessment at any time.

PECR

We only send these emails to corporate subscribers, meaning limited companies and LLPs, at their business addresses. We do not cold email sole traders or ordinary partnerships. Every email says who we are and gives a simple way to opt out.

How we send

Our outreach emails are plain text. They carry no tracking pixels and no click-tracking links.

How long we keep it

If you do not reply, we delete your outreach record no later than 12 months after our last email to you. If you opt out, we keep only your email address, company and the date on a suppression list, so that we never contact you again.

How to opt out

Reply "stop" to any email from us, or write to desk@freightdocdesk.com.

We stop all further outreach to you, normally the same working day, and cancel any follow-up already scheduled. Opting out costs nothing and you do not need to give a reason.

B. Client pilot data

This part applies to the file details UK road forwarders share with us so we can chase signed PODs and CMRs from their hauliers.

Signed DPA first

No file details are shared with us until a data processing agreement under UK GDPR Art. 28 is signed by both sides, with EU GDPR Art. 28 terms added where they apply.

What we handle

  • File references, haulier names and the haulier's office, traffic or accounts contact details.
  • Names, work emails and work phone numbers of haulier office staff and of our client's Traffic and Accounts staff.
  • Driver names and vehicle or trailer registrations shown on paperwork.
  • Signatures, stamps and printed names on PODs and CMRs, and consignee names printed on documents.

We do not ask for or store National Insurance numbers, driving licence numbers, bank or card details, or medical data. If any arrive by mistake, we tell the client and delete them within 2 working days.

What we use it for

Only to chase and check delivery paperwork for that client, on that client's instructions. We never use a client's hauliers, customers, consignees, lanes or rates for our own sales outreach, and we never contact a client's customers or consignees.

Security

Encrypted storage and transport, named-user access with 2-factor login, a separate mailbox and storage for each client, and an access log. Everyone with access is bound by confidentiality. We tell the client about any personal data breach or unauthorised access without undue delay and within 48 hours of becoming aware of it.

Sub-processors

We use a small number of service providers to do the work: an email provider, a file storage provider, a language model provider used by the automated assistant, and a hosting provider. Each one is named, with its location, in the signed DPA before any data is shared, and each is bound by data protection terms at least as strict as ours. We give 14 days' notice of any change, and the client can object. We never sell client data and never pass it to anyone for their own use.

International transfers

The signed DPA lists where data is processed. UK data handled in the EEA is covered by the UK's adequacy regulations for the EEA. Data moving from the EU to the UK is covered by the European Commission's adequacy decisions for the UK, renewed in December 2025 and running to 27 December 2031. Any transfer outside the UK and EEA is named in the DPA with its transfer safeguard, such as the UK IDTA or Addendum, EU Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

Automation, in plain language

The chase emails are drafted, sent and checked by an automated assistant that runs on AI software. Our operations team supervises it and reviews every exception each working day. The assistant only requests and checks documents. It makes no payment, claim or compliance decisions, and no decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on anyone.

Deletion

We delete all client data within 30 days of the pilot ending or the plan being cancelled, and confirm the deletion in writing. If the client asks, we return the data first.

If you are a haulier who got a chase email

We wrote on behalf of the forwarder named in the email, about paperwork for a job you have already delivered for them. That forwarder is the controller of your data and we act on its instructions. Every chase email carries this footer:

Automated assistant for Your forwarder documentation desk, supervised by a human specialist. Reply HUMAN anytime.

Reply HUMAN to reach a person within 1 working day. If you ask us to stop, we send no more emails and tell the forwarder the same day. We never discuss rates, payment, waiting time, demurrage or claims with you.

This website

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking scripts and has no forms. It loads no outside fonts or scripts. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub processes standard technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the pages and protect the site from abuse.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct it if it is wrong.
  • Delete it.
  • Restrict how we use it.
  • Stop using it for direct marketing. This right is absolute and we always honour it.
  • Stop using it on the basis of legitimate interests.

Write to desk@freightdocdesk.com. We reply within one month. If your data was shared with us by one of our clients, we pass your request to that client and help them answer it within 5 working days.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.
