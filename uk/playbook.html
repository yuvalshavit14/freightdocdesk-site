Part 2

Copy-paste 3-email haulier chase sequence

Plain, polite and about one document. Send it to the haulier's office, traffic or accounts email. Replace every field in curly brackets before you send.

Email 1, Day 1

Polite first request

Subject: Signed CMR needed, our ref {File ref}, delivered {Delivery date}

Hello {Haulier contact name},

Thank you for running our job {File ref} from {Collection point} to {Delivery point}, delivered on {Delivery date}.

To invoice this job we still need the signed POD or the consignee-stamped CMR. Could you email a clear scan or phone photo of every page to this address?

For our Accounts team to accept it, it needs the consignee signature, the stamp or a printed name, the delivery date and our reference {File ref}.

Many thanks,
{Your name}
Traffic, {Your company}
Email 2, Day 3

Gentle follow-up

Subject: Following up, CMR for our ref {File ref}

Hello {Haulier contact name},

Following up on my email of {Day of first email} about job {File ref} ({Collection point} to {Delivery point}, delivered {Delivery date}).

Is the signed CMR in your office yet, or still with the driver in the cab? If the driver has it, a phone photo taken flat and in good light is fine. A scan from the depot works too.

If it will take a few days, just reply with a date and I will check back then.

Kind regards,
{Your name}
Traffic, {Your company}
Email 3, when it comes back unusable

Faint stamp, missing signature or wrong reference

Subject: One small fix needed on the CMR for {File ref}

Hello {Haulier contact name},

Thank you for sending the paperwork for {File ref}. Our Accounts team cannot accept it yet because {reason, for example: the consignee stamp is not legible, the second page is cut off, or it shows a different reference}.

Could you send a clearer copy of every page, or check with the driver for the consignee's stamped copy? A phone photo is fine.

Thanks again for your help,
{Your name}
Traffic, {Your company}

Before it goes to Accounts

5-item CMR and POD billing checklist

Check each document as it arrives. A CMR that misses one of these tends to come back from the customer, and the wait starts again.

  1. Consignee signature. Signed by the consignee at delivery (box 24 on the CMR), as well as by the driver.
  2. Stamp or printed name. A legible company stamp or printed name beside the signature.
  3. Delivery date. Visible, and matching the delivery date on the file.
  4. Every page, legible. Flat, in focus, nothing cut off. For a CMR, the copy the consignee signed and stamped.
  5. Your file reference. Matches the file in your system, so Accounts can match it to the sales invoice without guessing.

2-line message for Traffic to forward
