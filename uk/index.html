Where files usually stick

Nobody has done anything wrong. The CMR is just still in the cab.

Traffic is on live work

Ferries, Eurotunnel slots and customs holds come first. A third chase for last month's CMR rarely makes the list.

Accounts needs the stamped copy

No legible, consignee-stamped CMR or signed POD, no invoice. Completed files drift past month-end.

Most hauliers will send it

The paperwork is with the driver or at the depot. It needs someone to ask, politely, more than once.

Before you ask

We ask for paperwork. Nothing else.
