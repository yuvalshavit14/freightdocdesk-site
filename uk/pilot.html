How it runs

  1. 15-minute call

    Pick 10 completed files, 5 oldest open (within 6 months) and 5 from the last 30 days.

  2. Days 1 to 4

    Daily follow-ups from the next working day, coordinator copied. Faint, cropped, unsigned or wrong-ref copies go back.

  3. Day 5

    1-page written status on all 10: billing-accepted, promised with a date, or stuck with the exact reason.

  4. Days 6 to 10

    We follow up when a promised copy is late and keep chasing open files. Final count with Accounts on Day 10 (Monday to Friday, excluding UK bank holidays).

What billing-accepted means

The document Accounts can invoice against: consignee signature (CMR box 24), stamp or printed name, delivery date, every page legible, and your file reference on it. Accounts has the final word.

Which 10 files

Completed files held back from invoicing because the signed POD or CMR is missing, unstamped, illegible or shows the wrong reference. The 5 oldest still open (delivered within the last 6 months) and 5 from the last 30 days.

What we need per file

  • Your file reference, lane and delivery date
  • The haulier's name and their office, traffic or accounts email
  • What is wrong with the paperwork

Sending mailbox

A dedicated mailbox in your company's name, such as yourfirm@freightdocdesk.com. You can move it to your own domain in Month 1.

The exact footer on every haulier email

Automated assistant for Your Company documentation desk, supervised by a human specialist. Reply HUMAN anytime.

Chase emails are drafted, sent and checked by an automated assistant that runs on AI software. It only asks for documents and checks them. It makes no payment, claim or compliance decisions. A haulier who asks us to stop gets no more emails, and Traffic is told the same day.

Haulier and data firewall

  • Money stays with Traffic. No rates, payment terms, waiting time, demurrage, fuel surcharges or deductions. If a haulier raises one, we pass it to Traffic the same day.
  • Claims go to you. Damage, shortages and reservations noted on the CMR go straight to you. We accept no liability in your name.
  • Polite follow-ups only. Every email asks for the paperwork and nothing else.
  • No customer contact. If a copy can only come from the consignee, we tell you and you decide.
  • No system access. No TMS, accounts software, main inbox, haulier portal or bank.
  • No messages to drivers. For up to 3 WhatsApp or text-only hauliers, we draft a 2-line message and Traffic forwards it from their own phone.
  • Data. UK GDPR Art. 28 DPA signed by both sides first. Everything deleted within 30 days of the pilot ending or cancellation, confirmed in writing.

Fit checks in full

No open claim, shortage or damage on any of the 10, and no haulier you owe overdue or disputed money on other jobs. If Traffic or Accounts cannot make the call, we rebook. Your data rights and the ICO complaint route are in our privacy notice.
