How it runs

  1. Kickoff (15 min)

    Pick 10 delivered loads, 5 oldest open (under 6 months) and 5 from the last 30 days.

  2. Days 1 to 4

    Daily follow-ups from the next business day, coordinator copied. Blurry, cut-off, unsigned or wrong-load scans go back.

  3. Day 5

    1-page written status on all 10: billing-ready, promised with a date, or stuck with the exact reason.

  4. Days 6 to 10

    We follow up when a promised copy is late and keep chasing open loads. Final count with Billing on Day 10 (business days, Monday to Friday).

What billing-ready means
Receiver signature, printed name or stamp, delivery date, all pages, your load number, and the lumper receipt where your shipper needs one.
Which 10 loads
Delivered loads held from invoicing or factoring because the signed paperwork is missing, unsigned, blurry or shows the wrong load number. The 5 oldest still open (delivered under 6 months ago) and 5 from the last 30 days.
Intake list, 5 fields per load
  1. Load number
  2. Carrier name
  3. MC number
  4. Carrier billing or dispatch email
  5. What is wrong with the paperwork
Nothing else.
Sending mailbox
A dedicated mailbox in your company's name, such as yourbrokerage@freightdocdesk.com. You can move it to your own domain in Month 1.
How the software works
An automated assistant that runs on AI software drafts, sends and checks the daily follow-ups. Our operations team reviews every exception each business day. The assistant only asks for documents and checks them. It makes no payment, claim or compliance decisions. A carrier who asks us to stop gets no more emails, and your team is told the same day.
The exact footer on every carrier email

Automated assistant for Your Company billing desk, supervised by a human specialist. Reply HUMAN anytime.

Fit check, in full
No active cargo claim, shortage, damage or lumper dispute on any of the 10, and no carrier you owe overdue or disputed money on other loads. For up to 3 text-only owner-operators, we draft a 2-line message and your dispatcher forwards it from their own phone.
Carrier and data firewall
  • No talk of money with carriers. If a carrier raises it, it goes to your team the same day.
  • Cargo claims, shortages, overages and damage go straight to you.
  • Polite follow-ups for the paperwork and nothing else.
  • No contact with your shippers or receivers. If paperwork can only come from them, we tell you and you decide.
  • No claims, deviations or liability accepted in your name.
  • We never text drivers ourselves.
  • No access to your TMS, accounting software, ERP, main inbox, carrier portal or bank.
  • No load data before the NDA and Data Handling Addendum are signed. All of it deleted within 30 days of the pilot ending or cancellation, confirmed in writing.
