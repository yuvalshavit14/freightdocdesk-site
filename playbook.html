Part 2

Copy-paste 3-email carrier BOL and POD sequence

Plain, polite and about one document. No mention of pay, rates or deductions, so the carrier reads it as a paperwork request and nothing more. Send to the carrier's billing, dispatch or office email. Swap every field in curly braces before you send.

Email 1, Day 1

Polite first request

Subject: Signed POD needed, load {Load number}, delivered {Delivery date}

Hi {Carrier contact name},

Thanks for running load {Load number} from {Origin} to {Destination}, delivered {Delivery date}.

We still need the signed POD (or signed BOL) to bill this load. Could you email a clear scan or phone photo of every page to this address? If there was a lumper, please include the receipt too.

To be billing-ready it needs the receiver signature, printed name or stamp, the delivery date, and our load number on it.

Thank you,
{Your name}
{Your company} Carrier Billing
Email 2, Day 3

Follow-up

Subject: Following up, signed POD for load {Load number}

Hi {Carrier contact name},

Following up on my note from {Day of first email} about load {Load number} ({Origin} to {Destination}, delivered {Delivery date}).

Do you have the signed POD in the office yet, or is it still with the driver? If it is in the truck, a phone photo taken flat and in good light works fine.

If it will take a few days, just reply with a date and I will check back then.

Thanks,
{Your name}
{Your company} Carrier Billing
Email 3, when it comes back wrong

Blurry, missing signature or wrong load number

Subject: One fix needed on the POD for load {Load number}

Hi {Carrier contact name},

Thanks for sending the paperwork for load {Load number}. Our billing team cannot use it yet because {reason, for example: the receiver signature is missing, page 2 is cut off, or it shows a different load number}.

Could you send a clearer copy of every page, or check with the driver for the receiver's signed copy? A phone photo is fine.

Thanks again,
{Your name}
{Your company} Carrier Billing

Before it goes to Billing

5-item billing-ready checklist

Check every document the moment it lands. A POD that fails one of these bounces back from your shipper or your factor and the clock restarts.

  1. Receiver signature. The receiver's signature at delivery, in addition to the driver's.
  2. Printed name or stamp. A readable name or receiving stamp next to the signature.
  3. Delivery date. Visible and matching the delivery on file.
  4. All pages, readable. Every page of the BOL, flat, in focus, nothing cut off.
  5. Your load number. Matches the load in your TMS, plus the lumper receipt where the shipper needs one.

For text-only owner-operators

Some drivers never read email. Your dispatcher forwards this from their own phone.

Text message

Dispatcher forward
