Privacy

Privacy and Business Contact Notice

This notice explains, in plain English, what data FreightDocDesk handles, where it comes from, why we use it and how to make us stop. It covers our outreach to US freight brokerages, the load data our broker clients share with us during a pilot or monthly plan, and this website.

Last updated: November 26, 2026

The short version

  • We email named people at US freight brokerages about our carrier paperwork desk. We find their work details in public business sources, FMCSA public records and business contact data providers.
  • To stop, reply "stop" to any of our emails or write to desk@freightdocdesk.com. We stop all outreach to you.
  • Client load data is only handled under a signed NDA and Data Handling Addendum, used only to chase delivery paperwork, and deleted within 30 days of the pilot or plan ending.
  • We never sell personal data.

A. Business contact outreach

This part applies if you received a first email from us about FreightDocDesk. Our business outreach is sent from freightdocops.com and freightdocmail.com. This site, client replies and pilot operations use freightdocdesk.com.

What we hold about you

  • Your name, job title and the brokerage you work for.
  • Your work email address, and your work phone number where your company publishes it.
  • Company details such as an FMCSA MC or USDOT number.
  • The public source where we found the details, and the date.
  • A record of the emails we sent you and any reply, including an opt-out.

We do not hold home addresses, personal email addresses or any sensitive personal data.

Where it came from

We did not get your details from you directly. We collected them from publicly available business sources and from business contact data providers:

  • Public company websites, including team pages, published job listings, and published broker policies and carrier terms.
  • FMCSA public records, such as SAFER and Licensing and Insurance.
  • Business contact data providers Apollo (apollo.io) and Prospeo (prospeo.io), which supply and verify work email addresses and job titles.

Why we use it

We use these details to send a small number of plain, relevant emails to people whose role covers billing, carrier accounting, carrier operations or brokerage operations, about a service that recovers delivery paperwork for their business. We only write about work matters, keep volumes low and stop at once when asked.

CAN-SPAM

Every email uses accurate sender details and a truthful subject line, includes a valid physical postal address and offers a simple reply-to-stop route. We honor opt-outs straight away, well inside the 10 business days the law allows.

How we send

Our outreach emails are plain text. They carry no tracking pixels and no click-tracking links.

How long we keep it

If you do not reply, we delete your outreach record no later than 12 months after our last email to you. If you opt out, we keep only your email address, company and the date on a suppression list, so that we never contact you again.

How to opt out

Reply "stop" to any email from us, or write to desk@freightdocdesk.com.

We stop all further outreach to you, normally the same business day, and cancel any follow-up already scheduled. Opting out costs nothing and you do not need to give a reason.

B. Client pilot data

This part applies to the data our US freight broker clients share with us so we can chase signed BOLs, PODs and lumper receipts from their carriers.

Signed agreement first

No live client data is shared with us until an NDA and Data Handling Addendum is signed by both sides.

What we handle

  • Load numbers, carrier names, MC numbers and carrier PRO numbers.
  • Names, work emails and work phone numbers of carrier billing, dispatch and office staff and of our client's staff.
  • Driver names and truck or trailer numbers shown on paperwork.
  • Signatures, stamps and printed names on BOLs, PODs and lumper receipts, and shipper or receiver names printed on documents.

We do not ask for or store Social Security numbers, driver's license numbers, bank or card details, carrier W-9s, or medical or drug test data. If any arrive by mistake, we tell the client and delete them within 2 business days.

What we use it for

Only to chase and check delivery paperwork for that client, on that client's instructions. We never use a client's carriers, shippers, receivers, lanes or rates for our own sales outreach, and we never contact a client's shippers or receivers.

Security

Encrypted storage and transport, named-user access with 2-factor login, a separate mailbox and storage for each client, and an access log. Everyone with access is bound by confidentiality. We tell the client about any data breach or unauthorized access without undue delay and within 48 hours of becoming aware of it.

Service providers

We use a small number of service providers to do the work: an email provider, a file storage provider, a language model provider used by the automated assistant, and a hosting provider. Each one is named, with where it processes data, in the signed agreement before any data is shared, and each is bound by confidentiality and data protection terms at least as strict as ours. We give 14 days' notice of any change, and the client can object. We never sell client data and never pass it to anyone for their own use.

Automation, in plain language

The chase emails are drafted, sent and checked by an automated assistant that runs on AI software. Our operations team supervises it and reviews every exception each business day. The assistant only requests and checks documents. It makes no payment, claim, credit or carrier-compliance decisions.

Deletion

We delete all client data within 30 days of the pilot ending or the plan being cancelled, and confirm the deletion in writing. If the client asks, we return the data first.

If you are a carrier who got a chase email

We wrote on behalf of the freight broker named in the email, about paperwork for a load you already delivered for them. That broker controls your data and we act on its instructions. Every chase email carries this footer:

Automated assistant for Your broker billing desk, supervised by a human specialist. Reply HUMAN anytime.

Reply HUMAN to reach a human specialist within 1 business day. If you ask us to stop, we send no more emails and tell the broker the same day. We never discuss rates, pay, detention, lumper amounts or claims with you.

This website

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking scripts and has no forms. It loads no outside fonts or scripts. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub processes standard technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the pages and protect the site from abuse.

Your choices

Whatever state you are in, you can ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct it if it is wrong.
  • Delete it.
  • Stop all outreach to you. We always honor this.

Write to desk@freightdocdesk.com. We reply within 30 days. If your data was shared with us by one of our broker clients, we pass your request to that client and help them answer it within 5 business days.
